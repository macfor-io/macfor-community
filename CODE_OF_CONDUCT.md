# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want macfor to be a welcoming, productive community for everyone who wants
to contribute — regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Assume good intent; clarify before escalating.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks or insulting comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behaviour and will take fair and appropriate action in response to any conduct
they deem inappropriate. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Please report concerns to the maintainers by email at `conduct@macfor.io`. All
reports will be handled confidentially.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions — and to public spaces when an individual is representing the
project.
